Elara icon concepts

Three directions, each shown at 96 px for shape, 48 px for the launcher, and a true 24 px for the status bar and notification icon. Both field colours, because a launcher can show either.

Concept A — Refined Lunar

The existing crescent and gold dot, rebuilt. The crescent is now the difference of two circles on a fixed diagonal, so its two horns close at the same height and the limb keeps an even thickness; the dot sits on the crescent's own implied orbit instead of floating beside it. Same idea, better geometry, and it survives at 24 px because the limb is a mass rather than a line.

Concept B — Four Phases

The four phase colours as a ring of four equal arcs with matching gaps. Deliberately not a pie chart: the arcs are equal, unlabelled and round-capped, so it reads as a cycle in motion rather than as four measured values. At 24 px the gaps close into a soft four-colour annulus that still reads as a mark.

Concept C — Elara Monogram

An E whose spine is a shallow lunar arc. The bars are heavy and equal so the letter survives at 24 px, where it resolves as three marks beside a curve and still reads as an E. The single gold note is carried over from the current icon.

Before choosing

The current icon stays in place until one of these is approved; nothing in android/ has been touched. Whichever is chosen needs a monochrome variant for themed icons on Android 13+, which is drawn as a single-colour silhouette and cannot use the four-colour ring. Concept B therefore needs a separate fallback mark, while A and C convert directly. None of the three depict Greek mythology: the crescent is a moon, the ring is the app's own phase palette, and the monogram is a letter. The name's mythological origin is explained honestly in the README and is not claimed by the icon.
